From Nepal, for research

A Himalayan identity for an open digital future.

YNP combines the symbolism of Nepal's mountains, Dhaka patterns and temple traditions with an experimental approach to blockchain engineering.

This MVP is designed for local testing only. YNP test tokens have no monetary value and do not represent investments, securities, real currency or a public token sale.
